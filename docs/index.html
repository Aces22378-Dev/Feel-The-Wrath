PRIVACY POLICY

Feel The Wrath · Feel The Wrath Development · Last updated November 23, 2026

This explains what Feel The Wrath ("the game") stores about you, why, and what you can do about it.

What we store

  • Account: your username and your password. Passwords are never stored as text — only a salted, one-way hash (PBKDF2).
  • Recovery email (optional): only if you add one. It is used for one thing: sending you a code to reset your password.
  • Game progress: coins, research points, Silver Wraths, researched / owned / equipped weapons, your character, and codes you've redeemed.
  • PvP and ranked: match results (who played, who won, when), your Merit, wins, losses and any title you have.
  • Login sessions: a login token so you stay signed in (stored on the server only as a hash).
  • On your device only: settings (volume, keys, video) are saved locally and never sent to us.

What other players can see

Your username, character, rank, Merit, win/loss record and title can appear on the leaderboard, in lobbies and in matches.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare runs the game server and database. Like any web service it handles your IP address to deliver traffic and block abuse.
  • EmailJS and Gmail deliver password reset emails from feelthewrath.dev@gmail.com (only if you added an email).
  • GitHub hosts desktop updates; the desktop app checks it for new versions.
  • Discord (desktop app only): if Discord is open, the game shows what you're playing on your Discord profile using Rich Presence.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or send marketing email.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Settings → DATA → Delete Account permanently removes your account, progress, rank, title and email. Past match results may remain, but no longer linked to a username.

Children

The game isn't meant for children under 13. If you're under 13, don't create an account.

Your choices

  • Add, change or remove your recovery email in Settings → DATA.
  • Delete your account at any time in Settings → DATA.
  • Ask us about your data or this policy on our Discord: discord.gg/zEzJUzzAPj

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will be announced in the game or on our Discord.
